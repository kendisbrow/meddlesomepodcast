<p>
Short, sharp stories about the real work of running a small business — told by two operators who’ve lived it. No jargon. No theory. Just meddling. Jim and Ken have been business partners, and more importantly friends, for over thirty years.
</p>

#### Jim
<p>
Jim enjoys walking, travel, and sailing. If he's lucky his adventures allow him to do all of these things. Jim was a management consultant with big and small firms for most of his career. He currently resides in the Rocky Mountain region of the United States.
</p>

#### Ken

<p>
Landscaping, administrative assistance, the next pet rock (hey they can't all be winners), housing trusses, networking, software, and consulting. Ken has evaluated, started, run, reinvented, and disposed of numerous businesses. Sometimes he even made money doing so. Still insatiably curious and entrepreneurial at heart, he currently lives in the Pacific Northwest. 
</p>
